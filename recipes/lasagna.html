Lasagna

Description

An easy lasagna recipe that saves me lots of time. This dish satisfies everyone in the family. Try it tonight!

Prep time: 10 mins, Cook time: 1 hr 10 mins, Total time: 1 hr 20 mins.

Servings: 12

Ingredients

  • Ground beef (1 pound)
  • Spaghetti sauce (32 ounces)
  • Cottage cheese (32 ounces), mozzarella (3 cups), and Parmesan (1/2 cup)
  • Eggs (2)
  • Seasonings: dried parsley (2 tsp), salt, and black pepper
  • Lasagna noodles (9)
  • Water (1/2 cup)

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients and preheat the oven to 350 degrees F.
  2. Cook and stir ground beef in the hot (medium-high heat) skillet until browned and crumbly, 8 to 10 minutes. Drain and discard grease. Stir in spaghetti sauce and simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Combine cottage cheese, 2 cups of mozzarella chese, eggs, 1/2 of the grated Parmesan cheese, dried parsley, salt, and pepper in a large bowl.
  4. Spread 3/4 cup of sauce in a baking dish. Cover with 3 uncooked lasagna noodles, 1 and 3/4 cups of cheese mixture, and 1/4 cup sauce; repeat layers once more. Top with remaining 3 noodles, sauce, mozzarella, and Parmesan cheese. Pour 1/2 cup water along the edges of the dish. Cover tightly with aluminum foil.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven for 45 minutes. Uncover and bake for an additional 10 minutes. Let stand 10 minutes before serving.
